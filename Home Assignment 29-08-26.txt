Assignment 2 – Explore & Build an n8n Template
Open the Templates section in n8n and explore the available workflow templates (11,000+).

- Explore different workflows and understand what they are doing.
- Choose any one template/use case of your interest.
- Understand the complete workflow, nodes, integrations, and flow.
- Try to build/recreate the workflow yourself in n8n.

The objective is not just to copy the template, but to understand why each node is used and how the complete workflow solves a real-world problem.
